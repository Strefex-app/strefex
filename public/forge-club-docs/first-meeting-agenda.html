Founding Meeting

[DATE][LOCATION][START TIME] – [END TIME]

Purpose of this meeting: This is not a networking event. It is the moment this club comes into existence. Three outcomes: every founding member feels genuinely known; we leave with shared language around our values; we sign the Founder Pact together.

Facilitator Notes

On the opening round

This is the most important 30 minutes of the meeting. Resist the urge to rush it. If the conversation goes long, cut time from Practical matters — not from this. The depth of connection formed here determines the quality of everything that follows.

On the values discussion

Don’t treat the six values as fixed. If a member has a strong objection or wants to reframe something, explore it. The act of debating them together is more valuable than arriving at perfect wording.

On signing the pact

Make this moment feel significant. Read the pact aloud — ideally one section each — before signing. Take a photo of the signed document. Keep the original somewhere all members can access.

On practical matters

Keep this section short. The most important decision is: when do we meet next, and who is responsible for organising it? Everything else can be decided over a shared message thread.

Pre-Meeting Preparation

Send to all founding members at least 5 days in advance:

  • Read the Founder Pact and come prepared to discuss, challenge, or propose changes to any section.
  • Prepare a 2-minute answer to: ‘What brought you here, and what are you hoping this club becomes?’
  • Think of one specific thing — an introduction, a piece of knowledge, an opportunity — you can offer to another member in the first month.
  • Come with no agenda other than being present.
