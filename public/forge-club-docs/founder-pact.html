Preamble

We, the founding members of FORGE, come together not by chance but by choice. We share a belief that the most meaningful success is built alongside others — and that the most lasting impact comes from those willing to give before they receive.

This pact is not a legal contract. It is a statement of how we intend to treat each other and the world around us. By signing it, we commit to holding ourselves — and each other — to these principles for as long as we are members of this club.

This document belongs to all of us. It should be revisited, challenged, and refined as we grow.

Our Purpose

We exist to build a community of driven men who lead with integrity, grow relentlessly, and use their success as a platform for positive impact — in each other's lives, in business, and in society.

We are not a networking club. We are not a deal flow pipeline. We are a brotherhood built on trust, candour, and a shared commitment to something greater than individual achievement.

Our Six Commitments

Each founding member commits to the following, not as abstract ideals but as daily practice:

1

Impact before ego

I will measure my contribution to this club by what I give, not what I gain. I will lead with the question ‘How can I help?’ before ‘What is in this for me?’ I will celebrate the wins of others as generously as my own.

2

Brotherhood through trust

Everything shared within this club — professionally or personally — stays within this club. I will speak honestly to my fellow members because I trust them, and I will earn that same trust through my own consistency and discretion.

3

Lead by example

I will honour every commitment I make within this group. When I fall short, I will say so openly and without deflection. I will hold myself to the standards I expect of others.

4

Elevate relentlessly

I will bring my best thinking, experience, and networks to the table — not to impress, but to serve. I will remain open to challenge, feedback, and ideas that make me better. I will never stop growing.

5

Give without keeping score

I will share introductions, knowledge, and opportunities freely — before being asked, without expectation of return. I understand that the culture of generosity we build together is the club's most valuable asset.

6

Diverse minds, one mission

I will actively seek perspectives different from my own. I will listen before I speak, and I will treat the newest member's opinion with the same respect as the most experienced one's.

How We Hold Each Other Accountable

  • If a member falls short, any founding member may raise it — privately and respectfully — before the next meeting.
  • Unresolved concerns may be brought to the group for an honest conversation, conducted without blame and with the goal of repair, not punishment.
  • This pact is reviewed annually. Any member may propose amendments. Changes require unanimous agreement among founding members.
  • A member who consistently and deliberately acts against these values may be asked, by unanimous vote, to step back from the club.

Confidentiality

We operate under a strict Chatham House Rule:

  • Business matters, financial information, personal challenges, and relationships discussed within the club are not shared outside it — in any form, to any person.
  • This applies to casual conversation as much as formal discussions.
  • Members who inadvertently breach this rule are expected to acknowledge it immediately.
