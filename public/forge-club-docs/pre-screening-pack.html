Membership Application — A guide to what we ask of every applicant before the interview

This document sets out everything we ask before the interview takes place. If preparing these materials feels like a burden, that tells you something important.

Section 1 — Written Submissions

1.1 Personal Statement Required

400–600 words addressing: What brought you to apply? What do you want to give to this group — specifically? Which of the six core values do you find hardest to live by, and why?

1.2 Impact Statement Required

200–400 words. One specific instance in which you created a meaningful positive impact on a person, business, community, or cause. Not a list — a single, specific story with outcome.

1.3 Generosity Example Required

Max 200 words. One concrete thing you gave — an introduction, knowledge, an opportunity, your time — without expectation of return in the past six months.

1.4 Founding Vision Statement Founder Only

300–500 words. What does success look like for this club in five years? What standard would you hold a fellow founding member to? What would make you vote to remove a member?

Section 2 — Professional Documents

2.1 Professional Profile or CV Required

Current CV, LinkedIn profile export, or equivalent 1–2 page professional summary. Career trajectory, professional context, leadership and entrepreneurial roles.

2.2 Public Presence Recommended

Links to any public-facing content that reflects your professional or personal perspective. Max five links with one-line description of each. If you have no public presence, leave blank.

2.3 Current Venture or Organisation Overview Recommended

One-page summary of your current main professional activity. What stage is it at? What challenge are you currently working through? This is context for the interview, not a fundraising deck.

Section 3 — References

3.1 Peer Reference Required

One reference from a professional peer who has worked alongside you and can speak to your character in a collaborative setting. Must know you for at least two years. Not a family member.

3.2 Character Reference Required

One reference who can speak to your character outside of a professional context. Must have known you in a substantive capacity for at least three years.

3.3 Nominating Member Statement Founder Only

150–250 words from the sponsoring member: Why this person, and why now? Which values have you observed them living? What would you stake your own membership on?

Reference questions (sent in writing): (1) In what context have you known this person, and for how long? (2) Describe a moment when you saw this person choose integrity or generosity at personal cost. (3) Is there anything about this person’s character or conduct you would want a group of peers to know before admitting them?
