Application Decline

Two template variants — use the one appropriate to the candidate

Variant A — Standard decline (timing or fit)

[CITY], [DATE]

Dear [FIRST NAME],

Thank you for taking the time to go through our application process. We recognise that the materials we ask for require genuine thought, and we are grateful you gave them that.

After careful consideration, we will not be moving forward with your application at this time. This is not a reflection of your standing as a person or professional — it is a matter of fit and timing against the specific profile we are building for this founding cohort.

[OPTIONAL: One specific, honest sentence about what we valued in their application.]

We wish you well, and we mean that without qualification.

With respect,

[COORDINATING FOUNDER NAME]

Founding Member, FORGE

Variant B — Decline with door left open

[CITY], [DATE]

Dear [FIRST NAME],

Thank you for the thought and candour you brought to the application process. It reflects well on you.

We will not be moving forward at this stage. The founding cohort we are assembling requires a very specific balance of backgrounds and perspectives, and the timing is not right for your application to succeed in this round.

We would welcome a conversation in the future — not as a consolation, but because we mean it. If circumstances change on either side, the door remains open. We will reach out if we feel the moment is right. You are welcome to do the same.

With respect,

[COORDINATING FOUNDER NAME]

Founding Member, FORGE

Note: Personalise the placeholder fields before sending. Never send both variants to the same candidate.
